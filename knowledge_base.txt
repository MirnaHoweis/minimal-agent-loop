DOCUMENT: UAE Visa Policy
The UAE offers several visa types for visitors. Tourist visas are valid for 30 or 90 days. 
Citizens of 49 countries including the US, UK, and EU nations receive visa-on-arrival. 
Work visas require employer sponsorship and medical fitness tests. 
Student visas are tied to enrollment in accredited UAE institutions.

DOCUMENT: Abu Dhabi Weather Patterns  
Abu Dhabi has a desert climate with extremely hot summers. June to September sees temperatures 
above 40°C with high humidity. The coolest months are December to February, averaging 18-24°C.
Rainfall is rare, averaging only 42mm per year, mostly in winter months.

DOCUMENT: UAE Public Holidays 2025
New Year's Day falls on January 1. Eid Al Fitr lasts 3 days following Ramadan. 
National Day is celebrated on December 2 and 3. Islamic New Year date varies by lunar calendar.
Commemoration Day is observed on November 30. Arafat Day and Eid Al Adha follow the Hajj calendar.

DOCUMENT: Dubai Business Setup
Setting up a business in Dubai requires choosing between mainland and free zone registration.
Free zones offer 100% foreign ownership and zero corporate tax on qualifying income.
Mainland companies can trade anywhere in the UAE but require a local service agent for some activities.
The process typically takes 1-4 weeks depending on license type.

DOCUMENT: UAE Currency and Banking
The UAE Dirham (AED) is pegged to the US Dollar at 3.67 AED per USD.
Major banks include Emirates NBD, Abu Dhabi Commercial Bank, and First Abu Dhabi Bank.
Banking hours are typically Sunday to Thursday, 8am to 3pm.
ATMs are widely available and accept international cards with no restrictions.
